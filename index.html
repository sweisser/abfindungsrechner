From the table to the final wealth

The last “Wealth (end)” in the table is not the final wealth shown at the top. The table shows what your investments are worth before you sell them, in nominal euros. The final wealth assumes you sell everything at the end, pay tax on the gains not taxed yet, adds the value of the pension points earned (if counted), and converts the result into today’s money.

Expected vs. simulated. Results marked Expected come from one calculation with your main inputs, taken as the most likely values. Results marked Simulated come from many simulated futures: each one draws new-job timing, salaries and salary growth from the ranges under Uncertainty, and random yearly returns and inflation from the volatilities there. The Uncertainty inputs affect only the simulated results.

Simplifications. Annual assessment taxes (no payroll withholding timing); social security deducted fully as Vorsorgeaufwand; brackets and contribution ceilings not indexed over time; no Vorabpauschale; ALG I approximated as rate × net salary (Sperrzeit / Ruhen not modelled); pension entitlements not valued; severance is social-security free.
